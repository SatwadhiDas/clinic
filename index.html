Dr. Gaurav Singh

MBBS · MD Pediatrics, Safdarjung Hospital

Dr. Gaurav believes every child deserves care that is clinically sound and deeply kind. He trained at Safdarjung Hospital, New Delhi, and is a Consultant at Fortis Hospital, Vasant Kunj.

Parents know him for a calm, reassuring manner that puts children at ease. His focus is preventive care, vaccinations and early development, and on staying with families through every stage.
